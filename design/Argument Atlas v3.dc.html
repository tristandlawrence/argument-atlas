B · Reading room

Dark-first, parchment text, gold accent. Support and contradiction are a cool/warm pair rather than green/red. Why: people will spend long sessions reading dense argument here. A dark, low-glare ground signals study over scroll, and it looks like nothing else in civic tech. 4 · Review only where it's earned

Instead of gating structural edits everywhere, a claim can enter contested status when edit churn, revert rate or flags cross a threshold. Only then do non-moderator edits queue for review — and the status is public, with the reason stated.
